How progress works

QuizNote rewards practice in five ways — every one is earned by playing, and nothing is ever paywalled or locked behind purchases. Here's the full picture so you always know what you're working toward.

Your overall journey. Every round earns XP, which raises your Learner Level and rank — a measure of how much you've practiced across the whole app.

How XP is earned each round:

    Per-module depth. Each of the 35 modules has three difficulty levels; clear a level by scoring 85%+ accuracy in 2 rounds. Your medal shows the hardest level you've cleared. (You'll see these as colored dots on your journey map.)

    BronzeCleared the Easy level.
    SilverCleared the Medium level.
    GoldCleared the Tricky level — the module is fully mastered.

    Milestones for the things worth celebrating — your first round, perfect games, streaks, exploring new areas, and reaching new ranks. They fill your Collection on My Studio as you earn them.

    Consistency, gently encouraged.

    🔥
    Day streak — the number of days in a row you've practiced. Play at least one round each day to keep it growing. (Today counts the moment you play; miss a full day and it resets.)
    ◷
    Weekly goal — pick how many days a week you want to practice (3, 5, or daily). My Studio shows your progress toward it each week.

    Three small goals refresh every day on My Studio — quick wins that nudge you to mix things up.

    ✅
    Play to complete them — each quest is met just by playing (a round, a clean score, a Theory module, a longer round, and so on). They award bonus XP and reset at midnight.
    🌟
    One guaranteed win — the first quest each day is always "play a round," so you can start your streak and earn XP right away.

    No ads, no pay-to-win — every reward here is earned by practicing.
