01 Who We Are

QuizNote ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent music education project operating at quiznote.online. QuizNote is currently operated as an unincorporated project. A formal legal entity may be established in the future, at which point this policy will be updated accordingly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our web application (the "App").

02 What We Collect

QuizNote is designed to collect as little information as possible. Here's what may be collected when you use the App:

Type What it includes Why
Technical / usage data Browser type, operating system, approximate geographic region (country/state), referring URL, pages visited, and time of visit Collected automatically by our hosting provider (Vercel) to monitor uptime and performance. We do not use this to identify you.
Local game data Your quiz scores, streak counts, and selected settings (e.g., difficulty, instrument) Stored only in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.
Voluntary contact Your email address and message, if you contact us directly Used only to respond to your inquiry. Not added to any mailing list or shared with third parties.

We do not currently collect names, email addresses, or any account information during normal use of the App. There is no login, registration, or user account system at this time.

03 What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data — ever.
  • We do not display advertising or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not build a profile on you.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.
  • We do not share your information with third parties except as described in Section 5.

04 Cookies and Local Storage

QuizNote does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

We use browser local storage (not cookies) to save your game progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time through your browser's settings.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set minimal technical cookies necessary for the delivery and security of the App. These are not used to track you across other websites.

05 Third-Party Services

QuizNote relies on the following third-party infrastructure. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Vercel (hosting) — automatically receives standard server logs including IP addresses. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts may be loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not currently use analytics platforms, ad networks, or any other data-collecting third-party services beyond those listed above.

06 Children's Privacy

QuizNote is designed to be safe and appropriate for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information during normal use, no parental consent is required under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) for children under 13 to use the App.

If a child contacts us by email and we realize we have inadvertently received personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please contact us at [legal@quiznote.online].

07 Data Retention

Server logs collected by Vercel are typically retained for a short period (days to weeks) for operational purposes. We do not maintain our own long-term database of user data.

Local storage data lives on your device until you clear it via your browser settings. We have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

08 Your Rights

Because we collect so little data, most privacy rights are straightforward to exercise:

  • Access or deletion of game data: Clear your browser's local storage at any time — that's it.
  • Opting out of server logs: Use a VPN or privacy browser to minimize the technical data your device sends to servers.
  • Email inquiries: If you've contacted us by email and want that message deleted, email us and we'll remove it.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA respectively. Given that we collect minimal data, these rights are easy to fulfill. Contact us at [legal@quiznote.online] with any request.

09 Future Paid Features

QuizNote is currently free to use with no accounts or payments required. If paid features, user accounts, or subscription plans are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before launch to describe any new data practices — including what is collected at sign-up, how payment information is handled (by a third-party processor; we will never store card details ourselves), and how to delete your account.

You will be notified of material changes before they take effect.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes — particularly any that affect how we collect or use personal information — we will make the notice prominent on the App.

Your continued use of QuizNote after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
