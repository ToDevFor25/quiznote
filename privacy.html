01 Who We Are

QuizNote ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent music education project operating at quiznote.online. QuizNote is currently operated as an unincorporated project. A formal legal entity may be established in the future, at which point this policy will be updated.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our web application (the "App").

02 What We Collect

QuizNote is designed to collect as little information as possible. Here's what may be collected when you use the App:

TypeWhat it includesWhy
Technical / usage data Browser type, OS, approximate region, referring URL, pages visited, time of visit Collected automatically by Vercel (our host) for uptime and performance. Not used to identify you personally.
Local game data Quiz scores, streak counts, player display name, and settings (difficulty, instrument) Stored only in your browser's local storage. Never transmitted to us.
Voluntary contact Your email and message, if you contact us directly Used only to respond to your inquiry. Not added to any list or shared.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or account information during normal App use. There is no login or registration system at this time.

03 What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data — ever.
  • We do not display advertising or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not build a profile on you.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.
  • We do not share your information with third parties except as described in Section 5.

04 Cookies and Local Storage

QuizNote does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

We use browser local storage to save your game progress, player display name, preferences, and settings on your device. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set minimal technical cookies necessary for the delivery and security of the App. These are not used to track you across other websites.

05 Third-Party Services

QuizNote relies on the following third-party infrastructure. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Vercel (hosting) — automatically receives standard server logs including IP addresses. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not currently use analytics platforms, ad networks, or any other data-collecting third-party services beyond those listed above.

06 Children's Privacy

QuizNote is designed to be safe and appropriate for all ages, including children. Because we do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information during normal use, no parental consent is required under COPPA for children under 13 to use the App.

The "What can I call you?" feature stores a chosen display name (e.g., a nickname) in the browser's local storage on the player's own device. This name is never sent to our servers.

If we inadvertently receive personal information from a child under 13 via email, we will delete it promptly. Parent or guardian concerns: [legal@quiznote.online].

07 Data Retention

Server logs collected by Vercel are retained for a short operational period (typically days to weeks). We do not maintain our own long-term database of user data.

Local storage data lives on your device until you clear it via your browser settings. We have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

08 Your Rights

  • Game data: Clear your browser's local storage at any time — that's it.
  • Server logs: Use a VPN or privacy-focused browser to minimize technical data sent to servers.
  • Email inquiries: Contact us and we'll delete any message you've sent us.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA. Contact us at [legal@quiznote.online] with any request.

09 Future Paid Features

QuizNote is currently free with no accounts or payments required. If paid features, user accounts, or subscriptions are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated before launch to describe any new data practices — including sign-up data, payment handling (via a trusted third-party processor; we will never store card details ourselves), and account deletion.

You will be notified of material changes before they take effect.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will make the notice prominent in the App before the change takes effect.

Continued use of QuizNote after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
