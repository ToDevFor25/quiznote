01 Who We Are

QuizNote ("we," "us," or "our") is an independent music education project operating at quiznote.online. QuizNote is currently operated as an unincorporated project. A formal legal entity may be established in the future, at which point this policy will be updated.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our web application (the "App").

02 What We Collect

QuizNote is designed to collect as little information as possible. Most of what is stored never leaves your device. Here's the full picture:

TypeWhat it includesWhere it lives / why
Profile data A randomly-generated profile ID, a nickname you choose, a color, an optional self-reported experience level, and timestamps for when the profile was created and last used Stored only in your browser's local storage. Never transmitted to us. You can have multiple profiles on one device, and you can delete any profile at any time from within the App.
Practice round log An append-only log of completed rounds: which profile, which module, difficulty tier, number of questions, number correct, time taken, and completion timestamp Stored only in your browser's local storage. Used by the App to show personal-best scores and (in the future) to recommend what to practice next. Not transmitted to us.
UI preferences Selected difficulty tier per module, sound on/off, timed-mode setting, and similar per-module UI state Stored only in your browser's local storage. Convenience only.
Technical / usage data Browser type, OS, approximate region, referring URL, pages visited, time of visit Collected automatically by Vercel (our host) for uptime and performance. Not used to identify you personally.
Voluntary contact Your email and message, if you contact us directly Used only to respond to your inquiry. Not added to any list or shared.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, or account information during normal App use. There is no login or registration system at this time. Nicknames are freely chosen and are not verified as real names.

03 What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data — ever.
  • We do not display advertising or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not build a profile on you.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.
  • We do not share your information with third parties except as described in Section 5.

04 Cookies and Local Storage

QuizNote does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

We use browser local storage on your device to save the profile data, practice round log, and UI preferences described in Section 2. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings, or by deleting individual profiles from within the App.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set minimal technical cookies necessary for the delivery and security of the App. These are not used to track you across other websites.

Some features are not yet available during beta. If and when we add optional account sign-in, payments, or cloud sync, those features will rely on third-party providers (such as a payments processor or identity provider) that process limited data under their own privacy policies. We will update this policy to describe any such processing before those features launch.

05 Third-Party Services

QuizNote relies on the following third-party infrastructure. Each has its own privacy practices:

  • Vercel (hosting) — automatically receives standard server logs including IP addresses. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Bravura music notation font — loaded from a content delivery network so that music symbols render correctly. The CDN may log your IP address.

06 Children's Privacy

QuizNote is designed to be safe and appropriate for learners of any age, including children.

The App does not collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, photographs, geolocation, or any other personally identifiable information from any user during normal use. All profile data — including the nickname a learner chooses and their record of completed practice rounds — is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to our servers.

If a learner is under 18, the App should be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. Profiles intended for use by a minor are owned and managed by the parent or guardian, not by the minor independently.

If we inadvertently receive personal information from a child via email or another contact channel, we will delete it promptly. Parents and guardians with concerns can reach us at [legal@quiznote.online].

07 Data Retention

Server logs collected by Vercel are retained for a short operational period (typically days to weeks). We do not maintain our own long-term database of user data.

Local storage data lives on your device until you clear it via your browser settings. We have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

08 Your Rights

  • Profile and practice data: Delete any profile from within the App, or clear your browser's local storage to remove everything at once.
  • Email inquiries: Contact us and we'll delete any message you've sent us.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA. Contact us at [legal@quiznote.online] with any request.

09 Future Paid Features

QuizNote is currently free with no accounts or payments required. If paid features, user accounts, or subscriptions are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated before launch to describe any new data practices — including sign-up data, payment handling (via a trusted third-party processor; we will never store card details ourselves), and account deletion.

You will be notified of material changes before they take effect.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will make the notice prominent in the App before the change takes effect.

Continued use of QuizNote after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
