"Welcome back" — 5 ideas

All auto-fade after ~2.5s and are dismissible. Tap each black bar to preview it live over the mock header. Pick one (or mix). Note: this fires only on a fresh login/landing, not on every Studio visit.

1 · Top toast (slides down, fades)▶ preview
A compact card drops from under the header. Least intrusive — the page is fully usable behind it. "Welcome back, Panda · 7-day streak 🔥". Auto-fades; × to dismiss.
2 · Full hero splash (fades through)▶ preview
A brief full-screen gradient moment with the level ring + "Welcome back, Panda". Most premium/ceremonial. Fades to reveal Studio. Tap anywhere to skip.
3 · Bottom slide-up pill▶ preview
Rises from the bottom (twin of the recap sheet, so it shares a language). "Welcome back, Panda — ready to play?" Auto-fades. Note: shares space with the Play bar.
4 · Masthead greeting (expands inline)▶ preview
A warm band unfurls just under the header, then collapses away after a beat. Feels built-in, not a pop-up. Quietest. "Welcome back, Panda 👋".
5 · Center card + confetti▶ preview
A centered card springs in with a small music-note confetti burst. Most celebratory/playful (good for kids). Dimmed scrim behind. Fades after a beat.

My lean: #1 (top toast) for daily use — warm but never in the way — possibly escalating to #5 only on milestone logins (new level reached while away). Tell me your pick.
