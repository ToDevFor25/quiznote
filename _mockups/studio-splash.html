QuizNote splash — 5 ideas

App-launch splash for return users (skips the marketing index). Each shows ~2s then fades into My Studio. Tap a bar to play the full splash (tap it / Skip to clear). All carry the QuizNote mark; some fold in the level ring from your old #2.

1 · Clean brand▶ play
Logo mark springs in, wordmark + tagline fade up. Calm, premium, fast. The safe Apple-style launch screen. Light cream.
2 · Gradient hero + level ring▶ play
Your #2, splashier: teal→grape gradient, the animated level ring (3), "Welcome back, Panda · 137 XP to Lvl 4". Most personal + ceremonial.
3 · Dark stage + glowing note▶ play
Dark radial backdrop, the mark glows + pulses, a loading bar sweeps. Sleekest / most "real app boot". Great on OLED.
4 · Staff sweep▶ play
A music staff with a light sweep crossing it behind the mark — a musical signature unique to QuizNote. Brand-distinctive.
5 · Confetti welcome▶ play
Branded chunky mark + music-note confetti + "Welcome back!". Warmest / most playful — best for kids. Sun→teal wash.

My lean: #1 daily (fast, never tiresome) → auto-escalate to #2 on a milestone (leveled up while away) and #5 on a streak-save day. Splash only fires on a fresh app open / first visit of the day — never mid-session.
