Round-end redesign — proposal

Consolidates the six overlapping signals into a clean ladder: stars = how this round went (the hero), then two distinct progress tracks — XP (you're growing) and medal/mastery (you're mastering this module). The old standalone Best-streak card is gone (it just echoed the personal-best streak); records now appear only as a celebratory badge when you beat them, not as permanent clutter. Score is a detail under the stars, not a competing trophy. 🔥 is reserved app-wide for the daily streak (home screen), so "streak" means one thing.
